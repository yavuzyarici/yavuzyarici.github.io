About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Georgia Institute of Technology, working in the OLIVES Lab under the mentorship of Prof. Ghassan AlRegib. My research focuses on machine learning, computer vision, and image processing, with particular emphasis on representation learning and human activity recognition.

Prior to joining Georgia Tech, I received my B.Sc. from Bilkent University in Ankara, Turkey. I am passionate about developing intelligent systems that can understand and interpret visual information, with applications in activity recognition, representation learning, and multimodal data processing.
